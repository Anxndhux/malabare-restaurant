Heritage Roots · Kowdiar Sanctuary
A Legacy Reimagined

Centuries of Spice,
Crafted with Soul.

At MALABARÉ, our kitchen is an altar to the ancient Malabar coast — where Arab seafaring dhows, Portuguese galleons, and Travancore royal kitchens wove an unforgettable tapestry of spice.

Nestled near the historic gates of Kowdiar Palace in Thiruvananthapuram, MALABARÉ revives legendary culinary rituals: the gentle dum of short-grain fragrant Kaima rice in seasoned brass cauldrons, the crackle of freshly ground Tellicherry black peppercorns, and the rich richness of cold-pressed coconut milk drawn that very morning.

Every dish honors our ancestral fisherfolk and spice planters — sourced directly from sustainable organic farms in Wayanad and the morning catch of Vizhinjam's deep Arabian waters.

“True Malabar cooking is not heat; it is an ancient dialogue between the ocean's bounty and the hill's wild spices.”

Culinary Director & Master Chef

Chef Faizal Moideen

Born into a multi-generational lineage of Thalassery culinary masters, Chef Faizal refined his craft across Michelin-pedigree kitchens in London and Dubai before returning to Kerala to bring MALABARÉ to life.

His culinary philosophy blends time-honored slow-cooking practices with modern technical refinement. From cultivating direct relationships with Vizhinjam divers to foraging rare wild Tellicherry black peppercorns, Chef Faizal ensures that every plate at MALABARÉ conveys the authentic soul of God's Own Country.
